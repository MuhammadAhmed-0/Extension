Privacy Policy

WhatsApp Number Privacy

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

WhatsApp Number Privacy is a Chrome browser extension designed to improve user privacy while using WhatsApp Web.

The extension hides phone numbers, Zoom meeting links, meeting IDs, and passcodes displayed within the WhatsApp Web interface to help prevent accidental exposure of sensitive information, particularly during screen sharing.

2. Information Collection

The extension does not collect, sell, or transmit users' personal information to external servers.

3. Local Processing

The extension processes relevant content directly within the user's browser. Phone numbers, Zoom meeting links, meeting IDs, and passcodes are masked locally.

WhatsApp messages and contact information are not intentionally transmitted to the developer or any third party.

4. Storage

The extension uses Chrome's storage functionality to manage extension preferences and settings.

It may also support administrator-managed configuration through Chrome's managed storage functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

The extension does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer user information to third parties.

7. Security

The extension is designed to process information locally within the browser and minimize unnecessary access to user information.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or privacy practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the extension developer through the support contact information provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
